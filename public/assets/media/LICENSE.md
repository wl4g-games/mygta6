# Original in-game short videos

`street.webm`, `harbor.webm` and `night.webm` are seven-second recordings generated locally from OpenWorld City using `scripts/record-phone-media.mjs`. No third-party video or recorded music was copied. Audio consists of the game's original local ambience synthesis and nightclub music, captured with the scene. The scenes include CC0 models and surfaces attributed in `../characters`, `../vehicles`, `../city`, `../props`, and `../surfaces`. The map, brands and composition are original to this project. Media is loaded only when the PULSE app opens.
