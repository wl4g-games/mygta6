# City facade templates

Author / distributor: tevy. CC0 1.0 Universal, according to the individual source pages and embedded model metadata.

- https://tevy.dev/models/apartment-block — v2
- https://tevy.dev/models/office-tower — v2

Only LOD1 and LOD2 are bundled. `sources.json` preserves source versions, dimensions, download paths and SHA-256 hashes. Verified 2026-11-21. Source files are unmodified; the renderer bakes static transforms, batches compatible geometry by material, and instances the templates to fit deterministic city plots. Unavailable office / residential interiors are labeled private; OPEN shops use the game's actual generated interiors.
