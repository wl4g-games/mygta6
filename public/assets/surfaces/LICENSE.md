Only the 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are bundled. `sources.json` records original download URLs, exact sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Verified 2026-11-21. Files are unmodified. Their use in this game includes world-aligned UV projection and runtime material tinting.
