# Detailed vehicle templates

Author / distributor: tevy. License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as declared on the [asset library](https://tevy.dev/models) and individual model pages. These are generic unbranded models; in-game names, economy and behavior are original to OpenWorld City.

`sources.json` records each selected model page, source version, original dimensions and source-file SHA-256 hashes. Only LOD1 and LOD2 are bundled. Model bytes are unmodified; instances are resized, recolored, and their moving parts may be animated at runtime. Verified 2026-11-21.

The glTF assets use Meshopt geometry compression and Basis Universal / KTX2 texture compression. The Basis transcoder distribution in `../basis/` comes from Three.js's bundled third-party library; its upstream project is https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal (Apache-2.0).
